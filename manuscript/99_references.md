- Friedman, D. A. (2026). *Casino Royale: FIVE 00s — Special-Agent Mission
  Software.* PROJECT BOND film package
  (`projects/working/bond/casino_royale_1967`), local-only working tree.
- Shannon, C. E. (1948). *A Mathematical Theory of Communication.* The Bell
  System Technical Journal, 27(3), 379–423. — entropy / mutual information
  behind the identity-confusion model.
- Cover, T. M. & Thomas, J. A. (2006). *Elements of Information Theory*
  (2nd ed.). Wiley-Interscience. — the entropy, conditional-entropy and
  mutual-information identities the identity model computes.
- Strehl, A. & Ghosh, J. (2002). *Cluster Ensembles — A Knowledge Reuse
  Framework for Combining Multiple Partitions.* Journal of Machine Learning
  Research, 3, 583–617. — the normalized-mutual-information convention used
  for the routing NMI.
- Kemeny, J. G. & Snell, J. L. (1960). *Finite Markov Chains.* D. Van
  Nostrand. — absorbing-chain fundamentals behind the escalation ladder.
- Ethier, S. N. (2010). *The Doctrine of Chances: Probabilistic Aspects of
  Gambling.* Springer. — chemin de fer draw rules and baccarat odds behind
  the casino-table model.
- Shackleford, M. *Baccarat Basics.* Wizard of Odds. — the published
  infinite-deck baccarat probabilities that
  `deal_outcome_probabilities()` reproduces; the values reported in this
  manuscript are computed, not copied (see Results, *The casino table*).
- BOND-API protocol (2026). *The frozen MissionProvider contract.*
  `projects/working/bond/bond-api` (`src/bond_api/protocol.py`).
- *Casino Royale* (1967). Directed by Val Guest, Ken Hughes, John Huston,
  Joseph McGrath and Robert Parrish. Columbia Pictures. Source of the
